Privacy

Short version: this site does not collect your personal data.

All calculations happen in your browser. Text, numbers and passwords you enter are not sent to any server.

Your theme choice and the exams you add to the Exam Countdown are saved in your browser (local storage) on your device only. You can remove them by clearing your browser data.

If you later add analytics, ads or accounts, update this page to describe them.
